# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This applies in issues, pull requests, and any other
space around these repositories.

## Do

- Assume good faith and ask before you assume otherwise.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Be specific and brief. Say what you saw and what you would change.
- Accept that this is a solo project with limited time.

## Do not

- Harass, insult, or threaten anyone.
- Post private information about others.
- Post spam, ads, or off-topic content.
- Keep pushing after someone says no.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, lock threads, close issues and
pull requests, and block accounts that break these rules. Decisions are final.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment. You
can also open a task issue titled "Conduct concern" with no details, and I
will arrange a private way to talk.
