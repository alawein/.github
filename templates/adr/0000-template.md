# 0000. Short title that names the decision

- Status: proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

Status is `proposed`, `accepted`, or `superseded by NNNN`. Never rewrite an
accepted record. Write a new one, and change only the status line of the old.

## Context

What forced a choice. The facts a reader needs, no more.

## Decision

What we chose, in one or two sentences, in the present tense: "We use ...".

## Reasons

Why this option won. One line each.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and what we now have to keep doing.

## Rejected options

Each option we turned down, with the reason. Keep these so the idea does not
come back.
